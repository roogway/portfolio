Templates Are English-Only. Users Aren't.

Express has thousands of templates, mostly in English. Users in Japan, Brazil, Germany couldn't use them. Auto-Translate turned every English template into a starting point for anyone.

German Is 30% Longer Than English

Translation breaks layouts. German words are longer. Japanese reads vertically. The system reflows text to fit the original design without manual cleanup.

Bad Translations Happen

Users can flag issues and rate quality inline. This feeds back into the model. No surveys, no interruptions.

Context Changes Meaning (Patent Filed)

"Bow" could be a ribbon or a weapon. The LLM looks at the image to decide. This visual context system is now patent-pending.

The Experience

Pick what to translate

Select pages, text boxes, or everything. Keep your brand name in English, translate the rest. Choose formal or casual tone. Hit translate.

Runs in the background

Large files take time. Instead of a loading screen, translation happens while you work on something else. Come back when it's done.

Flag bad translations

Rate quality and report issues inline. Data feeds back into the model. No separate survey.

Layout stays intact

German is longer. Japanese is vertical. The text reflows to fit. No manual cleanup.
