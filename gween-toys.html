Gween Toys

Wooden toys made by Channapatna artisans in India. I designed 25 new toys in 2.5 months, working directly with craftspeople who use 300-year-old techniques.

Specs Without Words

Technical drawings didn't work. Artisans in Channapatna don't read English specs. I made visual docs with color-coded annotations and 3D renders they could follow without text.

Modular Systems

Wobots are mix-and-match robots. Dwino is a dinosaur that spells words. Parts swap between toys. More play from fewer molds.

Respect the Craft

Channapatna toys have a specific look: lacquer finish, turned wood. New designs had to use those techniques. No shortcuts.

No Wrong Way to Play

Kids figure it out themselves. Parents don't need instructions. Every toy works multiple ways.
