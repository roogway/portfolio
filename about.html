Hey, I'm Raghvi.

I'm fascinated by how humans interact with their environment. I enjoy diving deep into underlying systems and uncovering connections that make the solution feel like finding the missing piece of a puzzle. Good design, to me, comes from understanding things deeply enough that the answer feels obvious in hindsight.

Deep understanding usually means leaving your own well, borrowing from adjacent fields, testing with real people early, staying uncomfortable a little longer than feels natural. I relish getting lost in the details and exploring the unknown.

In my free time, I read, write, and find new ways to challenge my own viewpoints.
