Hi, I am Raghvi

I'm constantly fascinated by how humans interact with products, environments, and emotions. Good design, to me, comes from understanding things deeply enough that the answer feels obvious in hindsight. I can often be found rabbit-holing to unearth the connections that make a solution click.

That kind of understanding can only be found outside my own corner of design: in psychology, business strategy, even physics. It asks you to stay uncomfortable a little longer than feels natural. And break a few rules while at it.

I relish getting lost in the details when the time is right, and pushing the ceiling when it isn't. Asking what's possible if we dream a little bigger than what's practical, all while adding a bit of whimsy.

In my free time, I read, write, and look for new ways to challenge my own viewpoints.
