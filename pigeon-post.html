The Experience

Craft something personal

Choose a postcard design, write your heartfelt message, and schedule when it arrives. Pigeon Post handles the rest—delivering a moment of delight right to their inbox.

Pick a postcard

Browse through a curated collection of beautifully designed postcards. Each one sets a different mood—playful, heartfelt, or simply warm.

Write your message

No character limits, no read receipts. Just space to say what you really mean. Take your time—the recipient won't know it's coming.

Send it flying

Your pigeon takes off. The recipient gets a delightful notification, and the anticipation builds. Digital mail, but with soul.
